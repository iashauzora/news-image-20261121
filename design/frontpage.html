Manama · Lead Story

Iran Says It Struck U.S. Bases in Bahrain; Fire at Industrial Site

Tehran's Revolutionary Guards said they hit two American positions, at al-Riffa and Awali. Bahrain said the attack struck a civilian industrial facility and started a fire — no injuries — and that its defences intercepted other attacks, as the Gulf war widened for a second day.

Kryvyi Rih · Warsaw

Russian Missile Kills Two in Kryvyi Rih; Missiles Land in Poland

A ballistic missile tore into the centre of President Zelensky's hometown, wounding 58 people, six of them children. Two Russian missiles came down in south-eastern Poland — a "deliberate provocation," Warsaw said; NATO called it a grave violation.

Guasave · Sinaloa

Mexico Captures "Chapo Isidro," FBI Most-Wanted Cartel Boss

Special forces seized Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, on the FBI's ten most-wanted list, in a raid in Guasave. The arrest set off gunfights, fires and road blockades across Sinaloa, and at least 11 people died.

Antalya · Climate

COP31 Talks Run Into Overtime, Deadlocked Over Fossil Fuels

Nearly 200 nations at the UN climate summit in Antalya blew past their Friday deadline with no deal in sight, divided over a draft text that omits any mention of fossil fuels.

Junín · Peru

Strong Quake Rocks Central Peru; Lima Runs Into the Streets

A magnitude 6.7–6.9 earthquake deep beneath the Andes shook the capital at 0009 GMT Saturday, sending residents outdoors. Early reports pointed to minor damage and no deaths.

Brussels · Jakarta

EU and Indonesia Sign Trade Pacts After Ten Years of Talks

The European Commission confirmed the signature of the CEPA trade deal and an investment protection agreement with Indonesia, closing a decade of negotiation with Southeast Asia's largest economy — and opening one of the EU's biggest new markets.
